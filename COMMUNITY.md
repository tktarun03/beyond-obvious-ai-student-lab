## Your first useful contribution

| Time       | Contribution                                          | Done when                                                               |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 10 minutes | Fix a broken instruction or unclear sentence          | Someone else can follow the corrected step                              |
| 30 minutes | Reproduce a demo failure with synthetic evidence      | The issue includes demo ID, input, output and failed check              |
| 1 hour     | Add a Tamil or another-language companion explanation | A fluent reviewer confirms technical meaning and commands remain intact |
| 1–2 hours  | Add an edge case or improve accessibility             | The behavior is demonstrated and the relevant check passes              |
| Half a day | Publish a small prompt comparison                     | Inputs, settings, failures, rubric and limitations are available        |

These are suggestions, not claims that GitHub issues or labels have already been created.
Ask about a larger change before investing significant effort. Small, complete improvements
are welcome; contribution quality is not measured by lines added or the number of stars.

## Help others discover the lab

If this helped you, star the repository, share its link with a student or college coding
club, and show the exercise you actually tried. Fork it for your own learning and send
improvements back. Please do not use bots, spam, purchased stars or unearned endorsements.

Reusable introduction:

> Beyond the Obvious AI Student Lab is an open learning repo by Arunkumar Thamilarasu. Try ten engineering prompt exercises with synthetic inputs and answer-review checks, then build on five AI project scaffolds. Learn with examples from India and around the world; contribute a correction, translation or tested improvement.
